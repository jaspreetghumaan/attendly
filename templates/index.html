How Attendly Works

A simple three-step process to make classroom attendance faster, easier, and more organized.

  1. 1

    Generate a QR Code

    The teacher selects the class and subject, then starts an attendance session by generating a QR code.

  2. 2

    Scan to Check In

    Students scan the session QR code using a compatible device to submit their attendance.

  3. 3

    Record and Review

    Attendance records can be reviewed by the teacher, including student status and attendance summaries.
